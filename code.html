About Me

Engineering with Purpose & Reliability

I am a Computer Science graduate from the University of Management and Technology (UMT) in Lahore. Over the past few years, I’ve found my sweet spot building at the intersection of practical machine learning, asynchronous backend systems, and data engineering.

I got started with data science by digging into real, messy datasets — like analyzing thousands of police crime records during my internship at Knowatech. That experience taught me that the hardest part of applied AI isn’t downloading a model; it’s building reliable data ingestion, cleaning noisy inputs, and ensuring systems behave predictably in the real world.

Today, I focus on building complete AI-driven systems end to end. Whether that means architecting a sub-second RAG search over legal dossiers, streaming real-time crime incidents to police dispatchers over WebSockets, or pairing deterministic query engines like DuckDB with LLMs to eliminate numerical hallucinations.

What I Value in Code

Math in databases, reasoning in models

Models should never do mental arithmetic. I delegate aggregations to DuckDB or SQL where accuracy is 100%, and let LLMs do what they’re great at — language and reasoning.

Traceable & verifiable answers

In sensitive domains like legal or law enforcement, confidence without citations is dangerous. Every generation needs direct source chunk grounding.

Full-stack accountability

I take pride in owning features all the way through — from database migrations and OCR parsing to async API endpoints and crisp UI interactions.
